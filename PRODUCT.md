# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Evaluators browse a small persisted catalogue of games and inspect individual game details during the take-home assignment. They need to compare stored scores, platforms, developers, review summaries, and related games quickly without relying on a live source.

## Product Purpose

GamePulse presents the locally stored game catalogue and detail records as a clear evaluator-facing product experience. Success is a fast, trustworthy reading path from discovery and filtering to a complete stored game record.

## Brand Personality

Modern, calm, visually expressive. The interface should make dense game data feel intentional and easy to scan rather than theatrical.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic Bootstrap or admin-dashboard styling, neon gamer aesthetics, and excessive animation.

## Design Principles

- Make the stored record and its limits clear without treating missing data as an error.
- Give score, platform, and developer information a dependable visual hierarchy.
- Keep discovery controls familiar, direct, and keyboard-friendly.
- Use visual expression to support scanning, not to compete with game data.
- Preserve an offline-first reading experience with no required external asset request.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast expectations, preserve semantic HTML and keyboard access with visible focus, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
